Readmellow

Read webpages aloud, at your own pace.

Listen to selected text or an article with sentence highlighting, adjustable speed, and a compact player. Pop the player into its own window on supported desktop versions of Microsoft Edge.

Privacy policyGet support

Start listening

  1. Open a webpage and choose the Readmellow toolbar icon.
  2. Choose Read page, or select text and choose Read selection.
  3. Use the compact bar to pause, stop, change speed, or pop out the player.

Right-click → Readmellow: Read from here starts near the pointer. While reading a page, Option/Alt+click a sentence to jump there. The separate player docks back into the page when its native window is closed.

Privacy by default

Online voices are off by default. Readmellow uses voices reported as local by Microsoft Edge. Optional online voices may send reading text to the browser’s speech provider; enable them only after reviewing the notice. The publisher receives no reading-text uploads or usage analytics.

Requirements and limits

Use a current desktop version of Microsoft Edge. Pop-out needs browser support and a secure page, and the source tab and browser must remain open. Browser-protected pages, extension stores, the built-in PDF viewer, and image-only text cannot be read. Available voices depend on your device and browser. There is no guaranteed Natural/Neural voice catalog.
